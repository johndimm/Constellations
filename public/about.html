Constellations

Browse the collaboration graph of anything

Constellations browses the collaboration graph of anything. Start from a node — a movie, a person, an event — and follow the people who collaborated on it out to other nodes, wandering the network. The one constraint: the people need Wikipedia pages.

The graph is built on the fly from live language-model queries — no database; the whole graph exists nowhere. A local neighborhood is constructed around the node you pick and expands as you explore. D3 provides the force-directed layout, and images come from Wikimedia Commons.
